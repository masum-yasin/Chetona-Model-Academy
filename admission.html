Your Child’s Bright Future Begins Here

Given the right environment, caring guidance and encouragement, every child succeeds.

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes. Visit our Mirpur campus, meet our teachers in class, and see modern teaching at work — the caring environment families have trusted since 2006.

  • Our campus. Students, teachers and parents at the CMA gate — a community you can walk into and know.
