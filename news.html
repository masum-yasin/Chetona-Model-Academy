Play with purpose

Sports & Co-Curricular

Games and PE belong to every class — Bangla Medium and English Version share the same field, houses, and Sports Day.

Sports Programs

Physical education is on the weekly timetable. Football, cricket, badminton, and athletics run as seasonal programmes, with house teams from Class 3 upwards.

  • Football
  • Cricket
  • Badminton
  • Athletics
  • House teams

Annual Sports Events

Sports Day is a full-campus morning of track events, relays, and family attendance. Inter-house trophies are awarded the same afternoon in the auditorium.

  • Track events
  • Relays
  • House trophies
  • Families welcome

Learning beyond the classroom

Study Tour

Study tours give children stamina, curiosity and a wider view of the world — museums, science centres and historic sites, with the same care as a school day.

Outings with a purpose

Each tour is planned around the curriculum. Students keep notes, ask questions, and bring the visit back into class. Bangla Medium and English Version share the same trips, under staff supervision from departure to return.

  • 01
    Museums and heritage

    History and culture seen in person, not only in a textbook.

  • 02
    Science and discovery

    Labs, planetariums and exhibitions that turn a lesson into something they can stand in front of.

  • 03
    Safe travel

    Named staff, a published itinerary, and parent consent before every outing.
