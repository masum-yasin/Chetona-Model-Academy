Welcome to Chetona Model Academy

Inspiring Young Minds. Building Bright Futures.

For nearly two decades, Chetona Model Academy (CMA) has been nurturing children to grow into confident learners, compassionate individuals and responsible future leaders.

We provide a safe, caring and disciplined environment where academic excellence and public examination results go hand in hand with life skills.

Why Parents Choose CMA

Given the right environment, caring guidance and encouragement, every child succeeds.

CMA is one of Mirpur's respected educational institutions with two decades of experience. We combine learning with individual care, modern teaching and deep-rooted moral values.

Our students grow up with:

Strong Academic Foundation

A strong academic foundation that prepares students for lifelong success.

Character Development

Future-Ready Learning

Future-ready learning with digital literacy and STEM activities.

Caring Teachers

Caring teachers who know each child and work in partnership with parents.

Academic Programs Overview

English Version and Bangla Medium, from Pre-Play through SSC, with Science and Commerce groups at secondary level.

From Pre-Play to SSC on one campus

On this campus, families choose English Version or Bangla Medium. Children begin in Pre-Play and continue through SSC — one journey, one standard of care.

  • English Version and Bangla Medium — two streams, one campus
  • Pre-Play to SSC — Class X, Dhaka Education Board
  • Science and Commerce groups at secondary level
