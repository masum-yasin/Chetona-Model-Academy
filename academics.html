Academics

Academic Excellence. Learning That Inspires.

At CMA, education is more than excellent examination results. We help students develop the knowledge, skills and confidence through a well-researched curriculum where every day becomes inspiring.

Framework

Curriculum Overview

A clear picture of how CMA teaches — from first days in Pre-Play to SSC — in English Version and Bangla Medium.

How the CMA curriculum works

Lessons at CMA are more than exam preparation. Children build knowledge, skills and confidence every day through engaging classroom teaching, regular assessment, practical work and short learning games — so they are ready for the next test and for life beyond it.

  1. 01
    Early years (Pre-Play to Early III)

    An international early-years curriculum: play, phonics, first number work and a calm start to school.

  2. 02
    Class 1 to SSC

    National curriculum in English Version or Bangla Medium, on one campus, with the same care in every class.

  3. 03
    Secondary groups

    Science and Commerce prepare students for Dhaka Education Board SSC on this campus.

Language & Mathematics

Spoken English and structured phonics from the early years. Singapore Mathematics and a modern Math Lab so children understand, enjoy and stay a step ahead.

Science & Social Studies

Students observe, experiment and explore. Lab work links classroom ideas to the real world; social studies builds civic sense and a wider view of Bangladesh and beyond.

Arts & Physical Education

Art, music and sport sit on the weekly timetable. They are part of a full education — not extras after the “real” work is done.

Digital & future-ready

Early digital literacy, age-appropriate coding logic and foundational AI awareness in a one-student, one-computer lab.

Help to succeed

Academic Support

If a lesson is hard, extra help is ready — in class, in the library, or in a quiet one-to-one talk.

Every child can get help

With about 13 students to each teacher, we notice who is flying and who needs another explanation. Help is available in Bangla or English. We talk with parents early, so no child is left guessing.

  • In class. Teachers slow down, try a new example, or sit beside a student until the idea is clear.
  • After class. The library, study rooms and extra subject sessions are there for practice.
  • When a child is worried. A counsellor or learning-support teacher offers a calm, private conversation.
