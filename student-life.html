Parents & Student Life

Student life where every day is inspiring

Growing through experience — learning beyond the classroom. Wide-ranging extra-curricular activities from academic competitions to cultural programmes, science fairs to sports and study tours help children grow into well-rounded individuals.

Everyday school life

Uniform, timing and transport

The practical details families ask first — the same for Bangla Medium and English Version.

School uniform

Peach school shirts with navy bottoms, house badges and the CMA ID. A written uniform list is given at enrolment. Hair, shoes and winter layers follow the same code for every class.

School day

The campus day runs with assembly, lessons, break and activity time. Exact bell times are published with the class routine each session. Office hours and parent meetings are listed under Parent Contact Hours.

Transport

Supervised school vans with published Mirpur routes. Drivers and attendants are known to the office by name. Seats are limited and confirmed at enrolment.

Spaces

Facilities

Our secure, safe and CCTV-monitored campus features interactive smart classrooms, a dedicated library and a “One Student, One Computer” layout in our digital labs. A kids’ play zone and activity areas offer added flavour.

Laboratories

Modern mathematics and science laboratories, plus a computer lab with a one-student, one-computer layout and an AI Learning Centre.

Library

A dedicated library and reading spaces — quiet tables, story corners for younger children, and reference shelves for examination classes.

Sports Complex

Playground, kids’ play zone, indoor games space and activity areas for house matches and the annual sports meet.

Cafeteria

A simple, hygienic canteen with a short daily menu. Packed lunches from home are always welcome.

Auditorium

Assemblies, debates, prize-giving and cultural evenings share one hall — Bangla Medium and English Version on the same stage.

Smart Classrooms

Smart classrooms with interactive technology so lessons stay clear from Pre-Play to SSC.

Belonging

A School that Feels Like Home

Children learn best when they feel happy, safe and respected. Our teachers build caring relationships with students, creating classrooms where many classes are collaborative and competitive — creating enthusiasm, teamwork and leadership, where achievements are celebrated and every child feels valued.

This warm and supportive environment makes CMA more than a school — it becomes a second home.

How we live together

Code of Conduct

The same expectations for Bangla Medium and English Version — punctual, in uniform, courteous, and honest in work.

Student Code of Conduct

Students are expected to be on time, in uniform, polite in speech, and honest in work. Mobile phones are not used during the school day without permission. The full code is given at enrolment.

  • Punctual
  • Uniform
  • Courtesy
  • Honest work
  • No phones without permission

Disciplinary Policy

We prefer restoration over spectacle. Concerns go first to the class teacher, then to section heads. Serious matters are written down and shared with parents the same day.

  • Class teacher first
  • Then section heads
  • Written record
  • Parents informed same day

Voice and responsibility

Student Leadership

Leadership is service. Bangla Medium and English Version students share the same council, houses, and duties.

Student Council

Prefects and a student council are elected each session. They represent classes to the Principal, help at events, and set the tone we want in the corridors.

  • Prefects
  • Class representatives
  • Event support

Leadership Roles & Responsibilities

House captains, library monitors, and club secretaries have written role descriptions. Leadership here means showing up, listening, and doing the quiet work well.

  • House captains
  • Library monitors
  • Club secretaries

For families

Parent Contact Hours

Office hours and meeting times are the same for Bangla Medium and English Version families.

School Office Hours

Saturday to Thursday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The office is closed on Fridays and gazetted holidays.

  • Sat–Thu
  • 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
  • Closed Friday

Parent-Teacher Meeting Schedule

Formal PTMs are held once each term. Extra appointments with class teachers can be booked through the office. Please give 24 hours’ notice except in urgent cases.

  • Once a term
  • Book via office
  • 24 hours’ notice

Care

Health & Wellbeing

First aid and counselling — the same care for Bangla Medium and English Version students.

Medical Facilities

The health room offers first aid, rest, and a record of medicines parents have authorised. Emergency contacts are taken at enrolment and must stay up to date.

  • First aid
  • Rest room
  • Authorised medicine

Counselling Services

A school counsellor is available by appointment for students and, where it helps, for parents. Conversations are private, with safeguarding as the first duty.

  • By appointment
  • Discreet
  • Parents welcome
